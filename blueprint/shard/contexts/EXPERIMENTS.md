# EXPERIMENTS: Does society benefit from diverse 'experiments of living'?

## Parent context

The parent node argues that individuality is "one of the leading essentials of well-being". This node concerns the social return: whether the whole community, including those who have no wish to live differently, gains from letting others try different modes of life.
