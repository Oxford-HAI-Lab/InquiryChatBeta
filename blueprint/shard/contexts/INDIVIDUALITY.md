# INDIVIDUALITY: Is individuality essential to human well-being?

## Parent context

The central question asks when society may interfere with liberty. Ch. 3 gives the positive case for leaving self-regarding conduct free: individuality, the development of one's own character and mode of life, is not a luxury but "one of the leading essentials of well-being" (Ch. 3, para. 2).
